The deck

Eighty-one event cards in the Red Enamel frame, from Potsdam to Madrid. Each is a real event of the Cold War with its own title, effect and illustration, written for this game. Click a card to enlarge it.

Four kinds, four colours

Reading a card

Top left
The card's value, 1 to 4, for when it is spent rather than played as an event. A tally has a compass rose instead.
Top right
The era: one, two or three lit pips for the early, middle and late years.
Under the title
Whose event it is, and the era in words.
Rules
Bold words are the ones to scan for: where, how much, and what moves.
Last line
Whether the card leaves the deck once played or comes round again.

The texts use five terms: influence (a side's markers in a country), control (enough influence to hold it), Tension (one shared track; both sides lose if it tops out), prestige (the score) and tally (score a region now). There is no rules engine yet, so the numbers are a first draft.

One back for the whole deck

Black enamel and a gold compass rose: it takes no side, so a face-down card gives nothing away. Click it to turn it over.
