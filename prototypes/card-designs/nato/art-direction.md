# NATO — flag revision

The main requirement is immediate recognition: a large NATO flag with a clear white
compass rose, a simple blue field, and enough fabric texture to feel tangible.
There are no people in the current illustration. The front keeps the selected
frame's blue, brass, black title band and ivory rules, with a finer border and a
larger rectangular picture. Sunburst rays, the circular picture frame, the second
NATO badge, the long subtitle and the historical footer have been removed.
The title band is horizontal. The bottom corner number and EUROPE label have also
been removed, and the ivory rules panel extends down into their former row.

The shared neutral black/gold back remains the selected V2 01 back.

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: wide 3:2 illustration for the upper picture window of a minimalist NATO strategy-game card. Generate only the image, no card layout.
Primary request: an immediately recognizable NATO flag, simple and iconic, with just enough real fabric and natural light to feel tangible.
Subject: ONE deep navy/cobalt-blue NATO flag viewed nearly straight on, gently lifted by a light breeze. Its large, accurate white NATO emblem is unmistakably visible at the center: a four-point compass rose within a thin white circle, with thin white horizontal and vertical rays extending outwards. Correct NATO symbol, clean shapes and high contrast. The emblem must remain fully visible and close to undistorted; use only broad shallow folds.

No building, no people, no other flags, no competing focal points.
Style: restrained realistic fabric photography with a subtle painted finish appropriate for a premium historical board game. Soft daylight, deep blue cloth, crisp ivory-white emblem, gentle highlights along two or three broad fabric folds. Quiet, clear silhouette readable at thumbnail size. Keep the flag plane facing the viewer rather than receding into perspective.
